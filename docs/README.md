## Overview of Project Management Processes

OctoAcme follows a structured lifecycle approach to project management that spans five key phases: **Initiation**, **Planning**, **Execution**, **Release**, and **Closure & Retrospective**. 

During Initiation, teams validate business needs and stakeholder alignment by creating a lightweight Project One-pager that defines the problem statement, success metrics, and resource needs. Once approved, the Planning phase breaks work into shippable increments, establishes acceptance criteria, identifies dependencies and risks, and creates a prioritized backlog. The Execution phase is driven by a consistent team rhythm of daily standups, weekly delivery syncs, and sprint-based iterations tracked on GitHub Projects. Quality and Testing are foundational, with unit tests, integration tests, smoke tests for critical flows, and security scanning embedded in the CI pipeline. Release and Deployment are standardized through pre-release checklists, staged deployments, and rollback playbooks to reduce production risk. Finally, Retrospectives and Continuous Improvement are embedded after each sprint or milestone to capture learnings and convert them into actionable improvements.

OctoAcme's organizational structure centers on three core personas: **Project Managers** (PMs) coordinate delivery, manage schedules and risks, and ensure transparent communication; **Product Managers** (PdMs) define outcomes, prioritize backlogs, and measure success; and **Developers** implement features, write tests, and collaborate on design and quality. Communication is intentional and multi-layered—weekly syncs between PM and PdM, twice-weekly standups for delivery teams, monthly stakeholder updates, and ad-hoc escalations structured at three levels.

Risk management is continuous, with a Risk Register maintained throughout the project lifecycle. OctoAcme's core principles guide all action: customer-first priorities, iterative delivery, clear ownership, data-driven metrics, and continuous improvement culture that encourages feedback and learning.
